The 24th Awaji International Forum on Infection and Immunity

Call for Papers

Submission Period

May 8, 12:00 – June 10, 12:00 (JST)

Abstract Submission

  • The forum will be held on-site. Online presentations are not available.
  • Advance registration is required prior to submitting abstract.
  • Submit your paper on the abstract submission system.
    Language: English
    The length of a title should be 200 characters or less.
    The length of an abstract body should be 2,000 characters or less.

Program

Appointed Presentations（Review Talks、Symposia）

Review Talks and Symposia are presented by appointed speakers.
Presenters of regular presentations cannot choose these categories.

Regular Presentations (Poster)

We are taking applications for regular presentations.

[Presentation method]

Regular presentations are basically presented in posters.
A few poster presentations will be selected for “Poster Oral” (oral presentation) after an examination by the program committee.
When selected, you are to present both in poster and Oral.

[Presentation category]

Choose your presentation category from below.

  1. Viruses
  2. Bacteria
  3. Parasites
  4. Immunity
  5. Vaccine
  6. Others

[Selection results]

The results of selection will be announced via email in late July.
The program committee is responsible for selection of poster presentations.
